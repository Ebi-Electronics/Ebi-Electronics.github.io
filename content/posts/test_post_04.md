Title: Introduction to Sensors
Date: 2025-10-07
Category: Electronics

Sensors are devices that detect physical phenomena and convert them into electrical signals.

## Temperature Sensors

Temperature sensors come in various types:
- Thermistors (cheap, simple)
- DS18B20 (digital, accurate)
- DHT22 (temperature and humidity)

## Light Sensors

Photoresistors (LDR) change resistance based on light levels. They're perfect for building automatic lighting systems.

## Motion Sensors

PIR sensors detect infrared radiation from moving objects. Commonly used in security systems and automatic switches.

## Reading Sensors with Arduino

Use analog input pins (A0-A5) to read sensor values and process them in your sketches.
