Title: Building Your First LED Circuit
Date: 2025-10-08
Category: Tutorial

Let's build a simple LED circuit that you can control with a button.

## Components Needed

- Arduino microcontroller
- LED (any color)
- Resistor (220Ω to 1kΩ)
- Button switch
- Breadboard
- Jumper wires

## Circuit Diagram

Connect the LED to pin 13 through a resistor, and the button to pin 2. When the button is pressed, the LED lights up.

## Programming

Write a simple sketch that reads the button state and controls the LED accordingly. This teaches debouncing and digital input/output.
