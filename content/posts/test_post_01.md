Title: Getting Started with Arduino
Date: 2025-10-10
Category: Tutorial

Arduino is an open-source electronics platform that makes it easy to learn programming and electronics.

## What is Arduino?

Arduino is based on easy-to-use hardware and software. It's intended for anyone making interactive projects.

## Getting Your First Arduino Board

You can start with an Arduino Uno or Arduino Nano. Both are great for beginners. The Uno is larger and easier to work with, while the Nano is more compact.

## Your First Sketch

The simplest sketch is the "Blink" sketch, which makes an LED blink on and off. This teaches you the basics of programming and hardware interaction.
