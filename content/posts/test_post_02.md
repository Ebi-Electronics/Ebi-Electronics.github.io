Title: Understanding Microcontrollers
Date: 2025-10-09
Category: Electronics

Microcontrollers are tiny computers that can be embedded in almost anything.

## What Makes a Microcontroller Special?

Unlike regular computers, microcontrollers are designed to:
- Perform specific tasks
- Use minimal power
- Operate in harsh environments
- Be cost-effective

## Common Microcontroller Architectures

### ARM Cortex-M
Powerful and widely used in industry.

### AVR
Simple and beginner-friendly, used in Arduino boards.

### ESP32
Features WiFi and Bluetooth connectivity.
